# Business outlook

MINUTEMAN is first a personal build. This page records whether it could become a product. Each section
is short and honest: v0.1 has **no market validation**.

## Positioning
A DIY-buildable, open-interior aluminum hard-side shell for full-size trucks, sold as plans and/or a kit.
It sits below commercial empty shells ($17-21k, `competitors/modular_shells.md`) in cash cost and above
them in owner effort.

## Target customer
Tall people (6'4"+) who can't stand in most campers; owners who want to configure their own interior;
people with garage space and the patience to learn aluminum welding. Unvalidated.

## Market questions
- How many buyers would build from plans vs. pay for a kit vs. pay for a finished shell?
- Is "tall standing room" a real segment or one owner's need?
- Would buyers trust a DIY-welded structure on the highway? (Liability shapes everything here.)

## Manufacturing options
1. Plans only (lowest liability, lowest revenue).
2. Cut kit: CNC-cut sheet and pre-cut tube, owner assembles.
3. Kit with shop-welded nodes (removes the novice-welding risk).
4. Finished shell (requires a facility, certification questions, real insurance).

## Pricing model
Unknown. The reference points are $3.9-8k of materials and $17-21k commercial empty shells.

## Scalability
The parametric config makes other trucks a data change, not a redesign. Validation (testing, review)
does not scale for free.

## Risks
Structural liability; adhesive/joint durability; the novice-welding premise; weight over target; a small
niche; competitors already selling empty shells.

## Future products
High-low telescoping variant; larger cab-over; generic short/long-bed fitments; rail accessories.

**Status:** open questions only. Revisit after the first shell is built and validated (v1.0).
