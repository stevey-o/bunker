# Aluminum-framed campers

## Four Wheel Camper (FWC) Hawk: pop-up, welded aluminum frame
| | |
|---|---|
| Construction | Welded aluminum frame, one-piece aluminum roof, aluminum siding, soft-wall pop-up |
| Dry weight | ~1,100-1,200 lb depending on options (base model ~1,200 lb) |
| Price | Hawk Base slide-in from $19,995; full camper from $29,495; flatbed model from $41,995 |

Sources (accessed 2026-10-01):
- FWC Hawk base model: https://fourwheelcampers.com/model/hawk-model-pop-up-truck-camper-leader-full-size-trucks/hawk-base-model/
- FWC Hawk model page: https://fourwheelcampers.com/model/hawk-model-pop-up-truck-camper-leader-full-size-trucks/
- Truck Camper Adventure, owner review: https://www.truckcamperadventure.com/inside-the-four-wheel-camper-hawk-an-owners-honest-review/

**Lesson:** FWC proves a **welded aluminum frame** survives decades of off-road use. They weld the frame
in a factory with trained welders, though; MINUTEMAN limits owner welding to small nodes (ADR 0001).
The "base model" (shell with minimal interior) is FWC's equivalent of our shell-first concept.

## Alaskan Camper: hard-side, hydraulic telescoping
| | |
|---|---|
| Construction | Hard-sided walls that telescope hydraulically; lowers for travel, raises for standing room |
| 8 ft cabover | Dry weight 1,390-1,985 lb; MSRP $37,190-42,090 (buyer's guide range) |

Sources (accessed 2026-10-01):
- Truck Camper Magazine, Alaskan hard-side buyer's guide: https://www.truckcampermagazine.com/buyers-guide/alaskan-hard-side-buyers-guide/
- The Autopian, hydraulic telescoping overview: https://www.theautopian.com/this-truck-camper-is-both-a-pop-up-and-hard-sided-thanks-to-hydraulic-telescoping-tech

**Lesson:** Alaskan is the existence proof for the owner's "high-low" idea: hard sides with a low travel
height. It is also a reminder that the mechanism adds weight and cost. That is why high-low is a separate
future model (`camper_versions/future_concepts/high_low.md`) and does not shape v0.1.
