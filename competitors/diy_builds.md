# DIY builds

Owner-built aluminum campers mostly converge on these techniques:

- **Welded aluminum frames** need the right process, filler, and inert shielding gas. This is consistent
  with ADR 0001; several builders report that welding is the hardest skill to acquire.
- **Skin attachment:** builders have used 3M VHB tape as a structural bond between skin and frame, or
  polyurethane adhesive (Sikaflex 252) with 1/16 in nylon spacers to hold a controlled bondline. Others
  rivet skin to the frame with insulation in the wall cavity.
- Builders consistently underestimate time on sealing, doors, and windows.

## Lessons for MINUTEMAN
- **Controlled bondline thickness** (spacers) is how DIY builders get repeatable adhesive joints. Add it
  to the v0.2 adhesive test plan.
- VHB vs. polyurethane vs. structural acrylic (e.g., methacrylate) is an open decision. It belongs in
  `engineering/structural_questions.md` and needs coupon testing, not forum consensus.
