# Composite campers

## Total Composites: FRP sandwich shells and kits
| | Lynx 7' | Lynx 5' |
|---|---|---|
| Construction | Fiberglass-reinforced plastic skins, polyurethane foam core, up to R-16 | same |
| Dry weight | 606 lb | 540 lb |
| Price (empty shell) | $17,959 (2024) | $17,200 |
Models: Lynx, Wolf, Bobcat, Wolverine. European kits from EUR 11,970.

Source (accessed 2026-10-01): Truck Camper Magazine, Total Composites buyer's guide:
https://www.truckcampermagazine.com/buyers-guide/hard-side/total-composites-buyers-guide/

**Lesson:** A bonded sandwich panel shell is **much lighter** than a framed aluminum shell for the same
envelope. It is the main structural alternative to MINUTEMAN's approach. Its drawbacks for a DIY builder
are panel-joint engineering, repairability, and the attachment hard points that composites need. The
empty shell sells for $17-18k, which frames what "shell only" is worth commercially.

stove) matches MINUTEMAN's "leave the interior open" philosophy and validates a reinforced floor zone
for a portable power station (ADR 0003).
