# Structural questions (open, for v0.2)

1. **Floor:** sandwich (aluminum skins + foam core) or tube grid + plywood/composite deck? Which carries
   jack loads to the corners with the fewest welds?
2. **Corner and jack nodes:** weld size and HAZ-reduced capacity at 0.125-0.250 in wall; can a local shop
   weld these if the owner's practice coupons fail?
3. **Jack type and mounting:** bolt-on corner brackets vs. integrated jack boxes; swing-out clearance on an
   80 in body over a ~80 in truck.
4. **Tie-down system:** frame-mounted (e.g., Torklift / Happijac-style) anchors require camper tie-down
   points at specific heights and positions. Measure the truck first.
5. **Adhesive:** polyurethane (Sikaflex 252) vs. structural methacrylate vs. VHB. Choose by coupon tests
   (lap shear, peel, thermal cycling, -20 F to 160 F) plus 5052/6061 surface prep.
6. **Skin shear transfer:** bond width and rivet pitch for the racking diaphragm.
7. **Nose cantilever:** 24 in over the cab with a storage allowance; deflection limit under road input.
8. **T-slot rails:** slip and rotation capacity of drop-in T-nuts under vibration; rail-to-frame bolt pattern.
9. **CG and axle load:** where is the CG with a 150 lb power zone and 120 lb roof load? Is the rear GAWR
   respected? (Needs the payload sticker from the measurement sheet.)
10. **Fatigue:** welded nodes under road vibration; is a professional review needed before v1.0? (Assume yes.)
11. **Thermal bridging:** the frame is a continuous aluminum path through the insulation. Thermal break strategy?
12. **Galvanic isolation:** stainless fasteners, steel jacks and turnbuckles against aluminum.
