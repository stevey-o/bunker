# Future concept: high-low (telescoping) MINUTEMAN

**Status: concept note only. Does not influence v0.1.**

The owner wants both a low travel height and 6'7" standing room. A fixed hard-side cannot do both
(ADR 0002). A telescoping upper section can: Alaskan Campers has done it hydraulically for decades
(`competitors/aluminum_campers.md`).

## What it would take
- A rigid lower shell plus a sliding upper hard shell with a seal at the overlap.
- A lift mechanism (hydraulic, electric actuator, or gas-strut assist with manual lift) and locks at both positions.
- An overlap seal that survives dust, water, and cold. This is the hard engineering problem.
- Extra weight and mechanism cost: **not estimated.** An unsupported guess is 150-300 lb; do not use it for decisions.

## Payoff
Travel height could drop substantially (rough guess 20-30 in, depending on overlap and nose design), with the full 80 in interior when raised.
