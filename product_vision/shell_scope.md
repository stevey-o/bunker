# Shell scope

## In scope
Structural floor, aluminum frame, bonded aluminum skin, insulated walls and roof, interior protective
panel, one rear entry door, limited windows, four camper-jack attachment structures, truck tie-down
structures, interior T-slot utility rails, and *structural provisions* for a roof rack and a
battery/generator bay (ADR 0003).

## Explicitly out of scope
Shower, toilet, permanent bed, kitchen, sink, plumbing, water tanks, propane, refrigerator, permanent
cabinetry, electrical system, wiring, charge controllers, batteries, solar panels, HVAC, entertainment,
luxury finishes, and any assumption about interior layout.

## The boundary
The shell provides **places and paths**, not systems:

| Owner might add | Shell provides |
|---|---|
| Solar panels | Roof-rack bosses on crossmembers (RACK-ROOF-01..06) |
| Battery / power station | Reinforced 24 x 20 in floor zone with tie-downs, 150 lb reservation |
| Wiring | Reserved wire chase, front-left corner, floor to roof |
| Bed, shelves, desk, cabinets | T-slot rails UTIL-L/R/F; nose volume for storage/desk |
